Booking information

A booking request is not a confirmed booking and does not require payment. The artist must accept your request before deposit checkout is available. When enabled, checkout collects a 40% M-Pesa deposit with an 8% Valor fee on the deposit; the remaining 60% is paid directly to the artist.

Each service has its own cancellation policy. Review it before sending a request and again before deposit checkout. Approved refunds are reviewed under that policy and applicable law; contact Valor support for assistance.
