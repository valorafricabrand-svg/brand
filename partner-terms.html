WORKING DRAFT · VERSION draft-2026-11-21-v5

Valor Africa Partner Participation Agreement

For discussion and qualified Kenyan legal review only. This is not an approved or binding agreement. It summarizes the product flows currently built; it does not replace checkout disclosures, an event-specific contract, merchant terms, an investment agreement, or mandatory legal rights. Before use, complete every bracketed item, confirm the contracting entity and payment arrangements, and have counsel approve the terms. The dashboard acknowledgement records review of a draft only and is not contractual acceptance.

1. Parties, scope, and legal status

This draft is intended to describe participation between [insert Valor Africa’s complete registered legal entity name, registration number, and registered address] (“Valor”) and the individual or organization using a partner program (“Partner”). It covers event organizers, sponsors, creators/affiliates, artists and DJs, and event-branding inquiries. Gift-card and merchant-voucher sellers, employees, agents, and investors are not made parties to a commercial arrangement by this draft; they require their own approved terms or separately signed documents.

Partner is independent of Valor. Nothing here creates employment, agency, a franchise, joint venture, or authority to bind the other party. A person acting for an organization must have authority to do so. The official effective date, governing law, dispute forum, contracting details, and final terms remain [to be completed and approved by counsel].

2. Accounts and general responsibilities

Use an account you control, give accurate information, keep credentials private, and promptly correct material errors. Do not impersonate another person, misuse payment or referral systems, submit unlawful or infringing material, or interfere with the platform. You are responsible for your devices, account access, required business registrations, taxes, and professional or event licences that apply to your activity.

Applications and profiles may be published or activated automatically after required fields and basic validation pass. An application marked “approved” or “active” in a dashboard is not a promise of a commercial contract, custom deliverable, sponsorship, investment, or particular result.

3. Event organizers and ticketed events

Event listings are free to submit. Listings that pass required form and file validation may publish without a separate application review. The organizer is responsible for the event and its accurate title, description, date, location, capacity, ticket types, prices, entry conditions, permits, safety, accessibility, delivery, and attendee communications. Do not list an event or sell tickets unless you are authorized and able to fulfil it.

Ticket prices, payment methods, platform or processing fees, and any organizer settlement arrangements must be shown in the applicable checkout or separate written terms before purchase. Organizers must honour ticket purchases and applicable cancellation, rescheduling, refund, and consumer obligations. Valor provides platform and payment-processing functions; it does not guarantee attendance, sell-out, or event success. The published Refund Policy and any mandatory law apply to ticket orders.

4. Event promotion and advertising

Paid event-ad packages are sold by placement and duration. The available placement, number of days, KES price, and total are the values presented at checkout and recorded for that order. A confirmed purchase activates the selected placement for the paid period. Future package prices or availability may change, but do not change the recorded terms of an already-confirmed order.

Promotion provides the specified visibility placement only. It does not guarantee impressions, clicks, ticket sales, audience size, press coverage, or a particular return. If an ad is cancelled or removed for policy, legal, or operational reasons, that action does not itself constitute or process a refund; refund requests are reviewed under the applicable checkout terms, published policy, and mandatory law.

5. Event sponsorships

Where available at checkout, published sponsorship packages currently include Community (KES 25,000 for 14 days), City (KES 75,000 for 30 days), and Premium (KES 200,000 for 60 days). The exact package, price, duration, brand details, and payment method shown in the order govern that purchase. Sponsorship visibility begins after payment is confirmed and ends at the recorded end time. Valor may remove or pause content that is unlawful, misleading, unsafe, or inconsistent with platform rules.

A custom sponsor inquiry or an indicative tier selected on an inquiry form is only a request for follow-up. It is not an order, invoice, campaign approval, or commitment. Custom scope, placement, schedule, price, audience, and reporting must be agreed in a separate written proposal. Sponsorship is a purchase of visibility, not an investment in Valor and not a promise of commercial results.

6. Creator and affiliate referrals

An activated creator receives a tracked referral link. The standard commission is 5% of eligible paid ticket subtotals attributed to that link, excluding separately itemized payment fees; a different rate applies only if it is expressly approved and recorded for that creator. The system attributes the last valid referral click for up to 30 days. A creator cannot earn commission on their own account’s ticket orders. Cancelled, refunded, reversed, unpaid, or fraudulent orders do not qualify.

Dashboard figures are records of attributed activity, not guaranteed earnings. Valor records manual payouts after funds have been transferred; the dashboard entry does not initiate a transfer. Any payout schedule, minimum, tax treatment, and process for disputing a commission must be completed in the final terms or written payout notice. Creators must make legally required disclosures when promoting a paid referral and must not make misleading claims about an event or their relationship with Valor.

7. Artist and DJ profiles, services, and bookings

Artists and DJs may publish a public profile and multiple service listings with their own descriptions, prices, durations, and cancellation policies. The Partner is responsible for the accuracy of those details, availability, professional conduct, licences, and the performance of the service. An artist or DJ is an independent service provider, not a Valor employee or agent. A booking request is not confirmed until the artist accepts it; availability conflicts or a paused profile may prevent acceptance.

For an accepted booking, when PayHero is configured and checkout is not paused, the organizer may pay a 40% M-Pesa deposit through Valor. Valor’s 8% fee is deducted from the deposit; the remaining deposit amount is recorded for manual artist payout after both artist and organizer confirm service completion. The organizer pays the remaining 60% of the service price directly to the artist, outside Valor. The exact KES amounts are shown in the booking dashboard before checkout. Valor does not automatically split or transfer the artist payout.

The organizer must review and accept the service-specific cancellation policy before paying. A cancellation after a deposit may enter support review; any approved refund and transfer are handled manually under the accepted policy and applicable law. A dashboard record of a payout or refund is not itself a transfer. Do not retry a payment that may already have completed; contact support for payment review. Booking updates may be emailed to the parties’ account email addresses as essential service messages.

8. Event branding and custom work

A branding inquiry, displayed example, or “from” price is not a confirmed order or final quote. Before work starts, the parties must agree in writing on the design, materials, quantities, specifications, approvals, price, payment or deposit schedule, delivery date and location, revisions, cancellation terms, and responsibility for production or delivery. Valor or the applicable supplier is responsible only for the deliverables expressly included in that accepted scope. No custom work is commissioned by submitting an inquiry alone.

9. Fees, payments, and records

All checkout prices and currency are shown before an order is submitted. Supported payment methods depend on configuration and may include M-Pesa through PayHero, Valor Wallet, card, crypto, or PayPal for particular products. Payment providers have their own terms and may impose processing times, limits, or fees. A payment is treated as complete only after Valor independently verifies the provider’s confirmation. Do not share a PIN, password, or one-time code with Valor or another Partner.

Where this platform provides a manual payout or refund record, an authorized administrator records a transfer reference after the transfer is made. Recording is not a payment instruction and does not send funds. Provider receipts, checkout records, accepted listing policies, and dashboard notices should be retained for support and reconciliation.

10. Cancellations, refunds, and chargebacks

Ticket orders, sponsorships, event advertising, artist services, and custom branding can have different cancellation and refund terms. Review the applicable policy and checkout disclosure before paying. The ticket Refund Policy does not determine the refund outcome for an artist service; the accepted service-specific policy and applicable law apply. Refund requests may require support review and verification. No provision here limits consumer or other rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

Partners must promptly cooperate with a payment investigation, chargeback, duplicate-payment report, or refund review and provide accurate supporting records. A chargeback or provider reversal may adjust the related order, commission, payout, or campaign status, subject to applicable law and the final written terms.

11. Gift merchants and voucher sellers

Gift-card and merchant-voucher features are separate from the partner programs above. A merchant must apply, pass Valor’s verification and approval, and accept separate merchant terms before listing or selling. The platform implementation is not legal or regulatory approval; these features must remain subject to the applicable launch gate and legal/payment review. The merchant is responsible for accurate offer terms, redemption instructions, inventory, customer fulfilment, and its own legal and tax obligations. Voucher codes may be one-time and are released only after confirmed payment. Seller payouts are manual and recorded after transfer; refunds require support review and may require the merchant to confirm an unused code. This draft does not approve any merchant or replace merchant-specific terms.

12. Investment inquiries

An investment inquiry is only a request for a private discussion. It is not an offer, solicitation, investment recommendation, acceptance of funds, promise of returns, or commitment to issue equity or debt. No valuation, security, share, revenue participation, repayment, minimum investment, or funding term is offered here. Any investment requires independent due diligence, applicable legal and financial advice, and separate final documents signed by authorized parties.

13. Content, intellectual property, and publicity

Each party retains its pre-existing intellectual property. You confirm that you own or have permission to use every name, logo, photograph, music recording, video, text, design, and other material you submit. You grant Valor a limited, non-exclusive permission to host, reproduce, resize, and display submitted materials only as reasonably needed to provide and promote the listing, profile, campaign, or service you requested. This permission ends when the relevant content is removed, subject to backups, legal retention, and already-distributed materials.

Do not use Valor’s name, logo, or marks to imply endorsement, partnership scope, or results beyond the specific program you joined. Any broader licence, co-branding, testimonial, or case-study use must be separately approved in writing.

14. Privacy and service communications

Personal and business information is handled under Valor’s Privacy Policy. Partner dashboards store account and program information needed to operate the services. For artist bookings this can include event details, messages, the accepted cancellation policy, M-Pesa phone number, provider references, receipt, amount, and booking status. Authorized staff may access records for support, moderation, payment verification, payout, refund, and legal compliance.

Valor may send essential service emails about requests, responses, payments, cancellations, completion, refunds, payouts, and account security. These are not marketing messages and may continue even if marketing preferences are changed. Keep account email and contact information current.

15. Prohibited conduct, moderation, and suspension

Do not use a program for unlawful, deceptive, discriminatory, unsafe, infringing, fraudulent, or abusive conduct; manipulate referrals or transactions; evade fees; misuse personal information; or misrepresent ticket, sponsorship, investment, or service outcomes. Valor may remove or pause a listing, profile, referral link, campaign, order, or account where reasonably necessary for safety, security, suspected fraud, non-payment, legal compliance, or breach of platform rules. Where practical, Valor will provide a reason and a way to contact support. Any action remains subject to mandatory law and applicable refund obligations.

16. Term, changes, and ending participation

Program-specific listings and paid services run for their displayed or separately agreed duration. Either party may stop using a free program subject to pending orders, confirmed bookings, payment disputes, and obligations that accrued before termination. Final terms must specify notice periods, handling of active orders, content removal, outstanding commissions, and survival of relevant clauses. Valor may update online program details for future transactions; a material change to final contractual terms must follow the agreed notice and acceptance process.

17. Warranties, liability, and force majeure

The final agreement must include counsel-approved warranties, indemnities, liability allocation and limits, exclusions, force-majeure treatment, and remedies that fit the actual contracting entity, payment flow, and mandatory consumer protections. This working draft does not settle those provisions and must not be used to waive statutory rights or as a substitute for an event-, sponsor-, artist-, or branding-specific contract.

18. Governing law and disputes

The governing law, competent courts or dispute-resolution process, escalation contacts, and applicable time limits must be confirmed by qualified Kenyan counsel and inserted before this agreement is used. The parties should first attempt to resolve operational issues through Valor support, without limiting any legal rights or remedies.

19. Electronic notices and acceptance

Partner applications and service notices are handled online. After final approval, the agreement should identify how notice is delivered, how a legally authorized signer accepts on behalf of a business, the effective time, and how the accepted version is retained. The current dashboard action only records acknowledgement of this working draft for review; it does not create an agreement or investment commitment.
