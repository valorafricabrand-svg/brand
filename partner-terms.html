Valor Africa Partner Participation Terms

Draft for legal review only. Not an official or binding agreement. The correct registered legal entity, governing law, payment/refund language, liability provisions, and final terms must be confirmed and approved by qualified counsel before this document is used as an official agreement. Acknowledging this draft in the dashboard records review only and does not create a contract or investment commitment.

1. Parties and purpose

This draft is intended for participation between the person or organization accepting the final terms (“Partner”) and [insert Valor Africa’s complete registered legal entity name, registration number, and address] (“Valor”). It describes a proposed online process for event sponsorship, organizer, creator/affiliate, artist/DJ, branding, and investment-interest programs. Replace all bracketed details and have the final terms reviewed before publication.

2. Accounts and authority

Each person must use their own Valor account and provide accurate contact and organization details. A person acting for an organization represents that they are authorized to submit its information and, after final terms are published, accept terms on its behalf. Keep account credentials private and promptly update inaccurate information.

3. Event sponsorship packages

A sponsor may purchase a published package through the dashboard using an available payment method. The package price, placement, and duration shown at checkout are the proposed deliverables. Placement starts only after payment is confirmed and runs for the displayed duration. Visibility does not guarantee attendance, ticket sales, reach, engagement, press coverage, or any particular result. A custom campaign is not agreed until both parties accept a separate written scope, schedule, and price. Refunds are subject to the applicable published refund policy and any mandatory law.

4. Organizers and event listings

Event submissions that pass required form and file validation publish automatically; no application review or meeting is required. Organizers remain responsible for the event, accurate listing details, required permits and insurance, attendee safety, fulfilment, and any organizer refund obligations. Fees, payment timing, and any additional services must be disclosed before purchase. Valor may remove content or a listing that is unlawful, unsafe, or inconsistent with platform rules.

5. Creators and affiliates

Creator accounts activate automatically after a complete application. Each new creator account receives a referral link and earns a 5% commission on eligible, attributed, paid ticket-order totals. The last valid referral click is attributed for 30 days. Cancelled, refunded, fraudulent, self-referred, or reversed orders do not qualify. Payouts are recorded in the creator dashboard. There is no guaranteed audience, booking, or income.

6. Artists, DJs, and branding services

Artist and DJ applications activate automatically after required information is submitted; activation does not guarantee a booking, sponsor, audience, or income. Branding requests are recorded automatically without an application review. Production, delivery dates, and prices must be shown online and accepted before work begins. Partners must have the rights needed for logos, images, music, names, and other materials they submit.

7. Investment interest is non-binding

An investment inquiry is recorded without an application approval step, but is only a request to begin a private discussion. It is not an offer to sell securities, a solicitation, an investment recommendation, a commitment to invest or accept investment, or a promise of returns. No equity, valuation, revenue share, repayment, minimum investment, or other investment term is offered by these program terms. Any investment requires independent due diligence, applicable legal and financial advice, and separate final documents signed by authorized parties.

8. Content and brand permissions

Partners retain ownership of materials they provide. They grant Valor a limited, non-exclusive permission to host, reproduce, resize, and display those materials as reasonably needed to provide the requested listing, profile, or paid campaign during its agreed term. Partners confirm they have the rights and permissions required for that use. Any additional use or license must be separately agreed.

9. Payments, cancellations, and records

Payment providers may apply their own terms, processing delays, or fees. Valor will show the applicable price before a published-package payment is submitted. Custom work and other commercial terms require separate written acceptance. Cancellations, refunds, and chargebacks are handled under the published refund policy, the specific written scope, and applicable law. Keep receipts and dashboard notices for your records.

10. Privacy and communications

Account and inquiry information is handled under Valor’s Privacy Policy. By using the dashboard, Partners may receive service communications about submissions, payments, review status, and fulfilment. Marketing preferences do not disable essential service messages.

11. Suspension and ending participation

Valor may pause a profile, listing, link, campaign, or dashboard feature where reasonably necessary for security, suspected fraud, legal compliance, non-payment, or a material breach. Where practical, Valor will provide a reason and a way to resolve the issue. Ending participation does not remove obligations or rights that accrued under a separately accepted final agreement.

12. Risk, liability, and disputes

These draft terms do not establish final warranties, exclusions, liability limits, indemnities, dispute procedures, or force-majeure rules. Those provisions, and any consumer-law protections that cannot be waived, must be completed and reviewed by qualified counsel for the confirmed contracting entity and jurisdiction before the terms become official.

13. Online notices and electronic acceptance

Valor intends routine applications, status updates, published-package checkout, and final term acceptance to be completed online without an in-person meeting. Once final terms are approved, the dashboard may record the account, signer name, version, and time of acceptance as an electronic record. The current dashboard action only acknowledges this draft for review; a new explicit acceptance must be requested after final terms are approved.

14. Draft status and contact

Draft version: draft-2026-11-19-v2. Proposed territory: Kenya, subject to confirmation by counsel. Questions about this draft can be sent to hello@valorafrica.co.ke. Do not make payment or investment decisions based on incomplete or bracketed terms.
