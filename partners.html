HELP & GUIDANCE

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about tickets, event listings, partner programs, referrals, and artist bookings.

How long does approval take?

There is no single review timeline for every program. Some flows are self-service: eligible creator and artist profiles can activate after the required details pass validation, and organizers can publish events once their listing is complete. Custom sponsorship proposals and other support requests are reviewed individually. Check your dashboard for updates; no fixed turnaround is promised.

Can I join more than one partnership program?

Yes. You can use more than one program, such as organizing an event and sharing a creator referral link. Each program has its own eligibility, workflow, costs, and terms; joining one does not automatically enroll you in another.

What does it cost to get started?

Submitting an event listing, joining the creator program, and creating an artist or DJ profile are free. Sponsorship packages, event promotion, and branding services are paid options. Review the current package details and the exact price shown before checkout; custom branding and campaigns need a confirmed written quote.

What are the payment terms and supported methods?

Payment terms vary by program. Review the price, currency, fees, cancellation terms, and any settlement details shown at checkout or in a written quote before paying. Available payment methods depend on the product and live provider configuration; check the options shown at checkout. Never share your M-Pesa PIN, password, or one-time code with anyone.

How do creator and affiliate referrals work?

The standard commission is 5% of eligible paid ticket subtotals attributed to your link, excluding separately itemized payment fees. Attribution uses the last valid referral click for up to 30 days. Unpaid, cancelled, refunded, reversed, fraudulent, or your own ticket orders do not qualify. Your dashboard shows referral activity and commission records; earnings are not guaranteed.

How and when are earnings or payouts sent?

Creator commissions are paid manually; a dashboard balance change does not trigger a transfer. The platform does not publish a fixed creator payout schedule or minimum, so contact Valor support to confirm current arrangements. Organizer settlement follows the terms shown for the event before checkout. A dashboard payout entry records a transfer; it does not initiate one.

Does a booking request confirm an artist or DJ?

No. The artist or DJ must accept the request before it is confirmed. When PayHero is configured and booking checkout is not paused, an accepted booking may proceed to a 40% M-Pesa deposit through Valor. An 8% Valor fee is deducted from the deposit, and the remaining 60% of the service price is paid directly by the organizer to the artist. Review the service's cancellation policy before checkout.

Is there a contract or commitment period?

It depends on the program. Free listings and creator participation do not have a paid package duration. Sponsorship placements run for the duration displayed for the selected package. Custom branding and campaigns require a separately agreed written scope and price. The partner-terms page is still a non-binding draft, and acknowledging it in a dashboard is not signing a contract.

Can I get a refund if I cannot attend an event?

Ticket purchases are generally final if your plans change. Different rules apply when an event is cancelled or changed, where event-specific terms or applicable law provide otherwise. Read the Refund Policy for eligibility and how to contact support.

What if I paid but did not receive my ticket?

Sign in and check My tickets. If the ticket is still missing, contact Valor support with your order ID and payment reference if available. Please contact us before placing another order so we can check whether your payment was completed.

Are branding or custom sponsorship inquiries confirmed orders?

No. An inquiry is a request, not an order or final quote. Confirm the scope, schedule, price, deliverables, and any payment terms in writing before production or campaign work begins.
