THE OPPORTUNITY

Start in Kenya. Build for event discovery everywhere.

Our starting market is Kenya: connect local organizers and audiences through relevant event discovery, trusted organizer relationships, and a reliable ticket-purchase experience. The longer-term ambition is to expand the model into other markets; we are not claiming current coverage across Africa or globally. An investor case should pair this thesis with independently sourced market sizing for the specific countries targeted.

Ticketing

Service fees on completed ticket sales, with pricing clearly agreed and disclosed.

Organizer tools

Potential paid plans or premium tools as organizer needs and product capabilities develop.

Visibility & sponsorship

Potential featured listings and event sponsorship packages, measured against agreed campaign goals.

These are potential revenue streams, not a representation that each is currently live or profitable. Payment processing costs, taxes, refunds, and operating expenses must be accounted for before assessing contribution margin.

FOR ANGEL AND STRATEGIC INVESTORS

A clear growth plan. No promised returns.

We’re looking for aligned investors who can help fund product development and focused customer acquisition. Capital should be tied to an agreed plan, measurable milestones, and regular reporting—not unsupported projections.

Build the product

Improve event discovery, organizer tools, ticket purchase, and the reliability of the customer experience.

Grow supply and demand

Bring more event organizers onto the platform and reach ticket buyers through focused local campaigns and partnerships.

Measure progress

Agree on a baseline and track active organizers, listed events, ticket conversion, repeat purchases, and acquisition cost.

Planning example only: Tailor the allocation to the actual raise, cost estimates, and diligence; it is not a current budget or forecast. Set milestones from Valor’s verified baseline—for example, a ticket-sales target over a defined period—and report progress against them. Do not present illustrative targets as achieved results.

Funding structures to evaluate: Explore an equity or convertible structure with a lead investor. A capped share of eligible ticket-fee revenue could be considered for smaller private backers only after cash-flow modeling and Kenyan legal review. No rate, repayment cap, or investment terms are offered on this page.

How a raise could work: Start with a lead investor and discuss an appropriate equity or convertible structure with qualified Kenyan legal and financial advisers. Set the amount, valuation or conversion terms, milestones, and reporting expectations through due diligence and written agreements. Any investment carries risk; returns are not guaranteed. This page is an invitation to discuss a potential private investment, not a public offer.
