Booking and payment information

Sending a request does not confirm a booking or require payment; the artist must accept it first. When M-Pesa checkout is available, the organizer pays a 40% deposit through Valor, including an 8% Valor fee on the deposit, and pays the remaining service balance directly to the artist. Review the service cancellation policy before checkout.
