WHERE IT BEGAN

Our story

Valor Africa started as a brand, not a ticket shop. It began with a simple belief: when people step outside and show up together, something wakes up in them. At a festival, a race, a fashion show, a community gathering or a night under open sky, we stop being spectators of our own lives. We become aware of who we are, what we can do, and who stands beside us.

Africa already has that energy everywhere. It’s in the crowds at a sunrise run, the stalls at a market day, the drums at a cultural night, the engines at a race weekend. But too often those moments stay local and unseen, hard to find and harder to join. Organizers pour their hearts into them with no easy way to reach people and sell tickets.

So we built the bridge. Valor Africa Events & Tickets brings discovery, hosting and ticketing into one place. You find what’s happening, pay in a way that suits you, M-Pesa and cards included, and walk in with a ticket on your phone. Organizers get a stage, tools and an audience, and a good event gets noticed beyond the city where it happens.

We believe grand awareness and self-awareness belong together. When Africans gather, celebrate and compete outdoors, the world pays attention, and it should.
