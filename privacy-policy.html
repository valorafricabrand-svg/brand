Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Valor Africa Events provides event discovery, ticketing, and organizer services. This notice explains the optional activity tracking, personalisation, and advertising measurement on this website, and the event-view history kept for signed-in accounts. It is not a complete description of the information processed when you use an account, wallet, or ticket service.

Your choice

The cookie notice offers two equal choices. “Accept and personalise” turns on everything described under “Activity and personalisation” and “Optional advertising cookies” below. “Reject optional cookies” turns all of it off, and the website works fully either way. We count how many people accept or reject each day, without any identifier, so we can report an acceptance rate; nothing else is recorded about people who reject.

Activity and personalisation

If you accept, this browser stores a random visitor ID and a visit ID (a new visit starts after 30 minutes of inactivity). We then record, against that visitor ID: the pages you open and the page you came from, searches you type into the event search boxes, category and city filters you choose, events you open, love, or share, the steps of ticket checkout you reach, the outcome shown on the order-status page (for example paid or failed), and that you signed in. Page addresses are stored without query details, except the event, category, or city name; order, ticket, and payment links are never stored with their access codes. If you are signed in, these records are also linked to your account. We do not record what you type into forms other than search boxes, and we do not record payment details.

We use this to understand how people find and buy tickets and to improve the website. Only authorised Valor administrators can see it, in aggregate reports such as top pages, popular searches, and where people leave checkout. We do not sell it or share individual records with sponsors or organizers. Activity records are deleted automatically after 13 months.

To power “Recently viewed” and “Recommended for you,” this browser also keeps a list of the last 12 events you opened and a count of the categories and cities you browse. That information stays on your device and is used only to choose which events to show you.

Event-view history for signed-in accounts

Separately from the optional choice above, when you are signed in and open a published event page, we record that your account viewed that event. This lets us send event reminder emails about events you looked at; every such email includes a one-click unsubscribe, and you can opt out of marketing emails in your account. Anonymous view counts per event are also kept for organizer statistics and contain no visitor identifier.

Optional advertising cookies

With your permission, this website may load Meta Pixel on public pages to measure visits, public event-page views, and ticket-checkout starts for advertising. After an order is confirmed as paid, the order-status page may also send a Purchase event containing the order amount in KES and an event identifier. Meta may receive information such as your browser or device identifiers, IP address, the page you viewed, and interactions with public event content. Meta processes information under its own Privacy Policy.

Meta Pixel is not loaded unless you choose “Accept and personalise” in the cookie notice. Rejecting optional cookies does not prevent use of the website. Pixel tracking is not enabled on account, wallet, ticket, or organizer-dashboard pages. The order-status page does not send a PageView; it can send a Purchase event only for a confirmed paid order and only if you accepted optional cookies. Purchase events are deduplicated in this browser. Update this notice if your tracking practices change.

Sponsored placement measurement

On the homepage, an active sponsor placement may be counted as a platform-measured viewable impression after at least 50% of it remains visible on screen for one continuous second while the page is visible. The impression record includes the sponsorship, placement, and time, but does not include a visitor account ID. These measurements are not a guarantee of human attention. Aggregate impression totals are shared with the sponsoring account in its partner dashboard.

When someone follows a sponsor’s event link, Valor records the campaign, event, placement, and click time. A first-party browser-storage entry can retain that event referral for up to 30 days so a later ticket order for the same event can be attributed. The resulting order is linked to the campaign for aggregate reporting; sponsors do not receive buyer names, contact details, or individual order records.

Partner dashboard records

If you sign in and acknowledge the partner terms draft, we store your account ID, account email, the full name you entered, the draft version, and the acknowledgement time with your partner account record. This records which draft you reviewed; it is not a contract acceptance. Partner applications and sponsorship purchases are also associated with your account so you can view their status in the dashboard.

Artist profiles and booking requests

Artists and DJs who publish a profile provide a display name, profile type, city, bio, genres or specialties, image and portfolio links, social profile, and availability notes. Published profiles and service listings are visible to the public. When an organizer sends a booking request, we store the organizer account, event name, date, location, message, requested service, and the listing's cancellation policy so both parties can review the request and its status. For M-Pesa checkout, we also store the payment phone number, provider references, receipt, amount, and payment status. The organizer and artist can see booking details relevant to their request; authorized Valor administrators can access records needed for support, moderation, payment verification, and manual payout or refund records.

We send essential booking updates to the account email addresses of the artist and organizer, including new requests, responses, deposit outcomes, cancellations, completion confirmations, and recorded refunds or payouts. These are service messages, not marketing messages. Failed deliveries are stored and retried. Artist referral links use the existing creator referral process, which stores a referral token for attribution for up to 30 days.

Change or withdraw your choice

Use the “Cookie settings” button at the bottom of any public page to accept or reject optional cookies at any time. Your choice is saved in this browser’s local storage. If you withdraw consent after accepting, we stop recording activity, ask our server to delete all activity recorded under this browser’s visitor ID, clear the visitor ID and your “Recently viewed” and interest data from this browser, and instruct Meta Pixel to stop sending events. Deleting browser data also clears your saved choice. To ask about activity linked to your account, contact us using the address below.
